Beneficiary Category Information

APL (Above Poverty Line) – Limited subsidy benefits.

BPL (Below Poverty Line) – Eligible for subsidized essential commodities.

Antyodaya – Priority category with highest subsidy support.
Eligibility note: Reserved for the most vulnerable households identified by authorities.

Eligibility note (APL): Income-based verification applies.

Eligibility note (BPL): Poverty certification required.
